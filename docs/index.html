MyNotes+ Privacy Policy

Private. Encrypted. Yours.

Last updated: 25 July 2026

MyNotes+ is an offline‑first, privacy‑first note‑taking app. It is built on a simple principle: your notes belong to you and no one else — not even us. This policy explains what data the app handles (spoiler: as little as possible) and how it is protected.

Information we collect

None by default

MyNotes+ does not require an account, email, or login, and it does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising SDKs and no third‑party analytics or tracking libraries.

  • The content you create (notes, titles, checklists, colors, tags) is stored only on your device.
  • App settings (theme, toggles) are stored locally on your device and never transmitted.
  • We do not see, receive, or have access to your notes.

How your notes are stored

Every note is serialized and encrypted with AES‑256‑GCM before it is written to the on‑device database. The encryption key is generated and held in the Android Keystore (hardware‑backed on supported devices) and is used to decrypt content only in memory, only while the app is open.

Image and voice‑note attachments are also encrypted at rest with the same Keystore key. They are decrypted only in memory, on demand, when you view or play them, and are never kept as readable files on disk.

On‑device intelligence — text recognition (OCR) from images, handwriting‑to‑text for your drawings, note summaries on supported phones (via the device’s on‑device Gemini Nano), and the smart suggestions that spot dates, links, phone numbers, emails and addresses — runs entirely on your phone. Your note text, images and handwriting are processed locally and are never uploaded to any server. Some of these features download a small model once from Google (the download contains no personal data); OCR ships inside the app and needs no download. You can turn smart suggestions off in Settings.

Plaintext of your notes is never written to disk and never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable end‑to‑end encrypted Google Drive sync (see below), or you deliberately export a note or book — in which case the file is written only to your Downloads/MyNotes folder (or a different export folder you choose in Settings).

Because the key stays bound to your device and never leaves it, the encrypted note database and its attachments are excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and device‑to‑device transfer. This keeps your notes on the device that created them; it also means that installing the app on a new device (or restoring a backup) starts with an empty library rather than notes that could no longer be unlocked. Updating the app on the same device keeps all your existing notes and unlocks them normally.

Permissions we use

Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC) Used only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app with your fingerprint, face, or device screen lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by the Android system — the app never sees or stores it.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Used only when you tap Record voice note in the editor, to capture audio you attach to a note. Recording happens only on that explicit action while the app is open, and the audio is saved to the app's private, on-device storage. It is never uploaded.
Network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Used only if you turn on encrypted Google Drive sync, to upload and download your already‑encrypted notes to your own Google Drive. With sync off, the app makes no network connections at all.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Used only to show the reminders you create. Android asks for this the first time you set a reminder; if you decline, reminders simply won't show a notification. No other notifications are ever posted.
Alarms & reboot (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Used only to fire your reminders at the time you choose and to re‑arm them after the phone restarts. Reminder text is stored encrypted on your device; it only ever leaves the device as end‑to‑end encrypted data if you turn on Google Drive sync (see below).

The app requests no location or contacts permissions, and no camera permission of its own - photos are added through the Android system photo picker or camera app.

Optional end‑to‑end encrypted Google Drive sync

Off by default

Cloud sync is disabled by default and only ever runs if you deliberately turn it on and connect a Google account. When enabled, every note (of any type), your saved templates and your reminders are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so only unreadable ciphertext blobs are stored in your Google Drive.

The key that unlocks your notes is protected by a recovery passphrase that you choose and is kept only in wrapped (encrypted) form. Because the key is never uploaded in the clear, neither Google nor anyone you might accidentally share a Drive folder with can read your notes — only someone with your account and passphrase can. Signing in on a new device with the same account and passphrase restores everything.

Google Drive’s own privacy policy also applies to the encrypted blobs stored in your account. If you lose your recovery passphrase, the synced notes cannot be recovered — this is the trade‑off that keeps them private to you.

“Share a link” is separate and always a deliberate choice. When you tap Share → Share a link on a note, that single note is uploaded to your own Google Drive as a readable copy and given an “anyone with the link can view” permission so you can hand the link to someone. Unlike synced notes, this copy is not end‑to‑end encrypted — anyone who has the link can open it. Nothing is shared until you use this option, and you can revoke access at any time by deleting the file from the “MyNotes Shared” folder in your Google Drive.

“Share encrypted” is the private alternative. Choosing Share → Share encrypted packs the note into a passphrase‑locked .mynote file that is encrypted on your device before it leaves the app. It is not uploaded anywhere — you send the file through whatever app you like, and the recipient opens it inside MyNotes with the passphrase you gave them. Without that passphrase the file is unreadable, even to the app you sent it through.

No tracking, ads, or selling of data

  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not build advertising or user profiles.

Data retention & deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are in full control. You can delete any note (it moves to Trash and can be permanently removed), and uninstalling the app removes all of its local data. If you enabled cloud backup, delete the backup from your cloud storage account to remove the encrypted copies there.

Security

We use industry‑standard protections including AES‑256‑GCM encryption, the Android Keystore, and an optional biometric app lock. You can also enable “Hide in recent apps” to keep note previews out of the app switcher. No method of storage is 100% infallible, but MyNotes+ is engineered so that your content stays encrypted at rest and private to you.

Children’s privacy

MyNotes+ is a general‑purpose notes app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected, no data about children is collected.
