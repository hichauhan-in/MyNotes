MyNotes+ Privacy Policy

Private. Encrypted. Yours.

Last updated: 25 July 2026

MyNotes+ is an offline‑first, privacy‑first note‑taking app. It is built on a simple principle: your notes belong to you and no one else — not even us. This policy explains what data the app handles (spoiler: as little as possible) and how it is protected.

Information we collect

None by default

MyNotes+ does not require an account, email, or login, and it does not collect personal information. It contains no advertising SDKs and no third‑party analytics or tracking libraries.

  • The content you create (notes, titles, checklists, colors, tags) is stored only on your device.
  • App settings (theme, toggles) are stored locally on your device and never transmitted.
  • We do not see, receive, or have access to your notes.

How your notes are stored

Every note is serialized and encrypted with AES‑256‑GCM before it is written to the on‑device database. The encryption key is generated and held in the Android Keystore (hardware‑backed on supported devices) and is used to decrypt content only in memory, only while the app is open.

Plaintext of your notes is never written to disk and never leaves your device unless you explicitly enable encrypted cloud backup (see below).

Because the key stays bound to your device and never leaves it, the encrypted note database and its attachments are excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and device‑to‑device transfer. This keeps your notes on the device that created them; it also means that installing the app on a new device (or restoring a backup) starts with an empty library rather than notes that could no longer be unlocked. Updating the app on the same device keeps all your existing notes and unlocks them normally.

Permissions we use

Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC) Used only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app with your fingerprint, face, or device screen lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by the Android system — the app never sees or stores it.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Used only when you tap Record voice note in the editor, to capture audio you attach to a note. Recording happens only on that explicit action while the app is open, and the audio is saved to the app's private, on-device storage. It is never uploaded.

The app requests no location or contacts permissions, and no camera permission of its own - photos are added through the Android system photo picker or camera app.

Optional encrypted cloud backup

Off by default

Cloud backup is disabled by default and only ever runs if you deliberately turn it on. When enabled, MyNotes+ is designed to upload only encrypted data (ciphertext) to your chosen cloud storage. Your encryption keys never leave your device, so the cloud provider — and the developer — cannot read your notes.

If you enable cloud backup, the third‑party storage provider’s own privacy policy also applies to the encrypted blobs stored in your account.

No tracking, ads, or selling of data

  • We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • We do not show ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.
  • We do not build advertising or user profiles.

Data retention & deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are in full control. You can delete any note (it moves to Trash and can be permanently removed), and uninstalling the app removes all of its local data. If you enabled cloud backup, delete the backup from your cloud storage account to remove the encrypted copies there.

Security

We use industry‑standard protections including AES‑256‑GCM encryption, the Android Keystore, and an optional biometric app lock. You can also enable “Hide in recent apps” to keep note previews out of the app switcher. No method of storage is 100% infallible, but MyNotes+ is engineered so that your content stays encrypted at rest and private to you.

Children’s privacy

MyNotes+ is a general‑purpose notes app and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Since no personal data is collected, no data about children is collected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
